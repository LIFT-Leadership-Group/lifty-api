# Outreach rules

Status: supplied by David, 2026-09-18. These are now in
`src/agent-context/writing.md` (lifty-writing.v2).

## LinkedIn

Each message is one connected thought. The first sentence and the ask must
name the same job. Do not bolt a setup clause onto an unrelated question.

## Email

Every email uses six fields, in order:

1. Subject line
2. Address (`Hi` or `Hey` plus their name)
3. Opener (one line, specific to that email)
4. Main focus (core body, 2-3 sentences)
5. Hook (one sentence)
6. CTA

Field behavior:

- Subject line stays the same on all five emails so they read as replies.
- Address alternates: Hi, Hey, Hi, Hey, Hi.
- Opener is specific to that email.
- Main focus is the biggest variable.
- Hook and CTA vary.

Jobs by step:

- Emails 1-2: pain points or problems the lead may be experiencing.
- Emails 3-4: the client's products, offerings, or solutions for those pains.
- Email 5: final note, thank them for their time, ask for a different point of
  contact.
