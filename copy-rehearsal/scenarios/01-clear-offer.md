# Scenario 01: clear offer

Use this when the saved workspace already has a clear offer, audience, and
supporting evidence. Keep this input fixed after review so later drafts can be
compared with the baseline.

## Rehearsal request

The founder asks Lifty to recommend both LinkedIn and email copy. They have not
supplied their own templates. Show the recommended angle, then the full
templates.

## Saved company (fictional)

- Company: Palletwing
- Company type: early-stage B2B SaaS / tech, about $1.6M ARR, 9 people
- Why it might use Lifty: Maya is the founder-seller. She needs outbound to
  grocery owners, not inbound from a big brand team.
- Website: `https://www.palletwing.example` (founder-confirmed)
- Founder / sender: Maya Chen, founder
- One-line description: Palletwing shows independent grocers which SKUs are
  likely to stock out this week, before the truck is already late.
- Offer: a weekly SKU-risk list for each store, plus a 20-minute first-store
  walkthrough.
- Audience: owners and store managers of independent grocers with 2-12
  locations in the United States.
- Disqualifiers: national chains, restaurants, and software companies.
- Commercial voice: practical, store-floor language. Short sentences. Maya
  would not say "unlock," "seamless," or "platform." She talks about missing
  milk, not dashboards.
- Preferred CTA: "Want me to walk through one store's first weekly list?"
- Supporting evidence the founder supplied:
  - North Park Market cut emergency distributor orders from 18 a month to 4
    after 90 days with Palletwing.
  - The website describes a Monday SKU-risk list, not a full inventory rewrite.

The reviewed sample includes Jordan Hale, owner of Cedar Hill Market, a
